# Art licensing

The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the source code only. The art is not MIT: it
comes from the Liberated Pixel Cup community and keeps its original licenses.

## What's covered

- `public/assets/tiles.png`, `public/assets/props.png`: walls, floors, furniture and grave
  markers, packed from the packs listed below
- `public/assets/lpc/`: character layers from the
  [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
- `src/game/assets/*.json`: metadata and credits for the above

## Licenses

All of it is available under **CC-BY-SA** (3.0 or 4.0, per pack or layer), and you may use it
under those terms. Many pieces are also offered under GPL 2.0 or 3.0, or OGA-BY 3.0, as
alternatives. The exact license for each pack and each character layer is listed in the
in-game credits page (`/credits`), generated from `src/game/assets/credits.json` and the
`credits` field of `src/game/assets/lpc.json`.

project. Because they're packed into the same sheet as CC-BY-SA art, the sheet as a whole is
distributed under CC-BY-SA.

## If you reuse the art

Credit the artists as their licenses require (the `/credits` page and the packs' own credits
files have everything), and share any changes to the art under the same license.
